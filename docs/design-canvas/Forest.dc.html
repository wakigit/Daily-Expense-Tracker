Reduced motion

Triggered by the OS setting or an in app switch. Every loop stops on a composed frame. State changes swap in with a 0.25 s opacity fade instead of travel. The scene still shows the state, so nothing is lost.

Scene layers: decorative or state

State layers repeat what the number and label already say. Decorative layers say nothing about money and may be removed without losing information.
